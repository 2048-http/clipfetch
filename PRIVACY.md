# ClipFetch Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

ClipFetch is a Windows desktop client for parsing, previewing, and saving media from links supplied by the user. This policy explains what information is processed and why.

## Information we process

- Account information, including username, user ID, phone number or email address, avatar, account status, and membership information.
- Verification information, including email or SMS verification events, proof-of-work verification results, timestamps, and request IP addresses. Passwords are stored by the service only as irreversible hashes.
- Parsing and favorites data, including submitted links, source platform, title, author, cover image, media URLs, processing state, parsing history, and favorite relationships.
- Operational and security information, including request times, IP addresses, API errors, and security logs needed for troubleshooting, abuse prevention, and account protection.
- Local preferences, including download location, interface settings, and sign-in persistence options, which are primarily stored on the user's device.

## How information is used

Information is processed to create and secure accounts, complete sign-in and account recovery, parse user-supplied links, synchronize parsing history and favorites, send necessary verification messages, respond to feedback, prevent abuse, and maintain the service.

## Local storage and server synchronization

Downloaded media is saved to a location selected by the user. Interface preferences and download settings are normally stored locally. Account information, parsing history, and favorites are sent to and stored by the ClipFetch service at `xiaofi.cn` so that they can be displayed and managed after sign-in.

Signing out removes the current client sign-in state but does not automatically delete server-side account information, parsing history, or favorites.

## Third-party processing

The service may use SMS and email providers, cloud storage and content-delivery providers, and the source platform associated with a link submitted by the user. Only information needed for the requested feature is transmitted. Proof-of-work human verification is self-hosted and does not use advertising-based CAPTCHA tracking.

## Retention and security

Account information is retained while the account remains active and is handled according to the account-deletion process. Verification records expire after a limited period. Parsing history and favorites are retained until deleted by the user, the account is closed, or the service no longer requires them. HTTPS, password hashing, single-use verification codes, access controls, rate limiting, and human verification are used to reduce risk.

## User rights

Users can view account information, parsing history, and favorites in the application; correct editable profile information; delete parsing-history or favorite entries; sign out; request account deletion; and request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal-information processing.

## Contact

Questions and data-rights requests may be sent to `support@xiaofi.cn`.
